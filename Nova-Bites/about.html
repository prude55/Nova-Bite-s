Our Story

Nova Bites was founded by Chiamaka Eze, a chef who grew up between her grandmother's kitchen in Abakaliki and food trucks in Lagos. In 2026, she decided to bring both worlds together, blending timeless Nigerian recipes with the speed and creativity of global street food. Every dish on our menu tells two stories at once: where it comes from, and where it's going. Our mission is simple: bold, honest food, made fast, served with pride.
